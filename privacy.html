Noti Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Noti (“the app”) is a notification manager for Android by TaroThanTaro. This policy covers the app, including Noti Plus, its in-app purchase. It explains what data the app handles and what it does with it. In short: everything stays on your device.

Data the app accesses

When you grant Noti notification access in Android settings, Android delivers every notification posted on your device to Noti. A notification can contain personal information, such as message text, sender names, email subjects or calendar details. Noti reads:

  • the posting app’s package name, name and user profile (personal or work);
  • the notification’s title, text and expanded content, channel, category and time;
  • the notification’s tap action, so you can open it again from History.

Noti also reads the list of apps installed on your device that have a launcher icon, so you can pick apps for the filter and for alerts.

If you record an alert tone (Noti Plus), Noti uses the microphone only while you record it in the alert editor, for up to 5 seconds. If you make a spoken tone (Noti Plus), Noti gives the text you type to your device’s text-to-speech engine, the app chosen in Android’s text-to-speech settings (such as Speech Services by Google), which turns it into audio. That engine’s handling of the text is covered by its own privacy policy.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app’s features, on your device:

  • Filtering and blocking: deciding, according to your filter, whether a notification is shown or removed from the notification shade.
  • History: keeping a record of notifications so you can review and reopen them.
  • Alerts: playing the sound and vibration you configured for matching notifications, including tones you recorded or had spoken.
  • Matches (Noti Plus): comparing a notification’s title and text with the keywords you set, to decide whether a filter entry or an alert applies to it.

Storage and retention

History, the filter, alerts, matches, recorded and spoken tones, and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. A recorded or spoken tone is deleted once no alert uses it. History is deleted automatically after the period you choose in Settings (one month by default), and you can clear it at any time. All app data is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app’s data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

Purchases

Noti Plus is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play’s billing system. Google Play takes the payment in its own window; Noti never sees your payment details. Noti asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns Noti Plus, and keeps that answer (yes or no) on your device so Plus stays unlocked offline. Google’s handling of your purchase is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing

Noti does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and requires no account. Recordings never leave your device; the text of a spoken tone goes only to the text-to-speech engine described above. It includes Google Play’s billing library, used only to buy Noti Plus; the library’s diagnostics uploader is turned off, and without the internet permission it could not send anything.

Permissions

  • Notification access (granted by you in Android settings): to receive, block and record notifications.
  • Post notifications: to show a “Stop” control while an alert is ringing.
  • Vibrate: to play alert vibration patterns.
  • Microphone (asked the first time you record a tone): to record alert tones, only while you record one.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Noti Plus through Google Play.
  • View network connections: requested by Google Play’s billing library. It only tells whether the device is online; it gives no internet access.

Children

Noti is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process children’s data beyond what is shown in notifications on the device where it is installed.
